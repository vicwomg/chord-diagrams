How to Use Chord Diagrams

Editing Diagrams

  • Place / remove a dot: Left-click any string and fret position.
  • Fingering & highlight color: Right-click (or long-press on touchscreens) any fret position to set fingering (1–4, T) or switch the dot color between Black and Grey to highlight specific notes.
  • Open / muted strings: Click a string number or note name above the nut to cycle through O (open), X (muted), and blank.
  • Starting fret / nut: Leave the left-side fret box blank or 0 for a heavy black nut bar. Enter 1–24 to show a starting fret number on the left.
  • Labels: Click the page title or any chord name above a diagram to type a label.
  • Copy, paste & delete: Hover over any diagram to copy it, paste a copied chord into another box, or delete an individual diagram from the grid.

Layout, Saving & Printing

  • Instrument & frets: Switch between Guitar (6-string) and Ukulele (4-string), and toggle 5 frets for wider voicings.
  • Page density: Choose 4, 8, 12, 16, or 20 diagrams per sheet.
  • Saving: Your work-in-progress auto-saves in your browser across refreshes. Use Save to store named chord sheets and Open to load or manage them. Note: Saved sets are stored in browser local storage and will not survive clearing browser data.
  • Printing: Click Print to print or save as PDF without toolbar controls or input borders.
